ZP BrownSlope Prism

Passive analyzer: audio passes through untouched. It shows the spectrum with a 4.5 dB per octave slope compensation, so brown noise becomes a flat line. The two green lines are the -18 and -24 LUFS references, measured from the brown loops with the plugin's own calculation; the orange curve is your audio.

  • You are matched when the orange curve is flat and sits on the chosen line.
  • Readings: deviation in dB per zone (Lows, Low-mids, High-mids, Highs), residual slope (+ = brighter than brown, - = darker), level against the reference, result Matched / Close / No.
  • Shape removes the level difference and compares EQ only.
  • Controls: Average, Slope, Scale knobs (drag vertically; double click or right click = default); Ref., Shape, Reset, Calibrate, Default buttons.
  • Calibrate: play the reference loop with long average and press Calibrate; the curve is saved with the project. Default removes it.
  • JSFX view: brings back REAPER's parameter list, readings included, for OSARA and screen readers.
  • ? opens the guide inside the plugin; from there, GUIDA COMPLETA (full guide) opens this page (Cue Navigator must be running).

ZP BrownSlope Dynamic Guard

Advanced, visible version of the Brown module already inside ZP BUS Chain and ZP Master Pro: a 4-band dynamic tonal guard for the end of a voice/music chain, before the final limiter. It gently steers long-term band energy toward a brown slope, then applies optional broadcast HPF/LPF guards.

  • MATCH applies a gentle static slope toward the brown target.
  • TOL (tolerance) is a dead zone: the dynamic part acts only on real deviations.
  • BIAS chooses Cut-only, Balanced or Gentle Boost; MID Focus gives more weight to the low-mid and high-mid zones.
  • Stereo and M/S analysis: L, R, Mid, Side, Width and L/R difference. Correction stays L/R linked so the stereo image does not change.
  • Header: IN, OUT and W/D (dry/wet) tiles. The ? next to them opens this page (Cue Navigator must be running).

Status: not a beta yet. Do not use it on deliveries until it survives a real session.
