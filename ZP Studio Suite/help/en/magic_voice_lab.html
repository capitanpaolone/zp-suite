ZP Magic Voice LAB

Level a spoken take and tame resonances that stand out on particular words.

Start with a phrase you know

Insert ZP Magic Voice LAB on the voice track from the REAPER FX browser. Choose MAGIC 1 on the panel. Loop a phrase with a quiet ending, a loud vowel and a sharp consonant. Compare bypassed and processed at a similar listening level; use OUTPUT to compensate if needed.

If the low end booms

Choose FULL and ADVANCED. In MAGIC 1, band A covers 20–200 Hz. Click SOLO A to hear what falls in that range. Double-click its LP frequency, type 165 and press Enter to try a narrower band. Esc cancels; Shift-drag makes fine adjustments. Click SOLO OFF and listen to the whole phrase before changing THR A.

If consonants jump out

In MAGIC 1, band B covers 1,565–2,261 Hz and C covers 3,600–5,734 Hz. Audition each band separately, then switch SOLO OFF. Increase its THR control for stronger reduction; back it off if consonants lose definition. DRC Max Band GR is the largest reduction on a single band.

Level, tone and output

PEAK RED evens out changes in level. DYN EQ acts when the voice builds up; TONE SCL changes the strength of the chosen tonal curve. DYN BAL sets how strongly the low, mid and high ranges move towards the selected profile. MIX blends dry and processed voice. DELTA lets you audition the difference: turn it off before rendering. PEAK CEIL limits sample peaks in dBFS; use a separate true-peak meter if the delivery calls for dBTP.

Find your way around

Click a module title to show it in MONITOR. Hover over a control to read the help line below. MINI keeps the monitor in view; JSFX exposes the parameter list for keyboard and screen-reader use.
