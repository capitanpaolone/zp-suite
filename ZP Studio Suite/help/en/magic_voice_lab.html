ZP Magic Voice / ZP Magic Voice LAB

Level a spoken take and tame resonances that stand out on particular words.

Start with a phrase you know

Insert ZP Magic Voice (or ZP Magic Voice LAB) on the voice track from the REAPER FX browser. Choose MAGIC 1 on the panel. INPUT (-12..+12 dB, auto-compensated) drives the dynamic detectors without shifting overall output loudness. Loop a phrase with a quiet ending, a loud vowel and a sharp consonant. Compare bypassed and processed at a similar listening level; use OUTPUT to compensate if needed.

If the low end booms

Choose FULL. In MAGIC 1, band A covers 20–200 Hz. In DRC, SOLO is spring-loaded: click SOLO A to arm the active band, then hold the button or drag THR, FREQ or OCT above to audition that band only (it disengages on release and automatically disarms after 10 seconds). FREQ (logarithmic center Hz, double-click to type a value) and OCT (bandwidth in octaves with smooth SVF -3 dB skirts) move the underlying bounds together; Esc cancels and Shift-drag makes fine adjustments. Listen back to the whole phrase on release before increasing THR A.

If consonants jump out

In MAGIC 1, band B covers 1,565–2,261 Hz and C covers 3,600–5,734 Hz. Arm and audition each band with spring-loaded SOLO while sweeping FREQ, shaping width with OCT and setting THR (the DRC monitor shows each filter's live SVF bell curve), then release to check in context. Increase its THR control for stronger reduction; back it off if consonants lose definition. DRC Max Band GR is the largest reduction on a single band.

Level, tone and output

HW INS (zero-latency hardware insert ahead of filters and dynamics, enabled in MagicFlow-derived presets including MAGIC 1) and PREAMP share an internal 5-profile hardware signature bank selectable at the bottom of Panel 1 (GLUE, TUBE, IRON, BCAST, TAPE, slider58). The default GLUE profile is calibrated to MagicFlow AU (low-mid cleanup dip, silky roll-off above 13 kHz, 6-stage phase-aligned all-pass network through consonant presence, and odd-H3 transformer preamp with zero DC); the remaining four profiles provide RMS-matched alternative curves and harmonic signatures (H2/H3) with a DC blocker whenever even harmonics are active. PEAK RED evens out changes in level. DYN EQ acts when the voice builds up (5 phase-coherent bands); TONE SCL changes the strength of the chosen tonal curve (including MID BUMP). DYN BAL sets how strongly the low, mid and high ranges move towards the selected profile. MIX blends dry and processed voice (phase-aligned on the all-pass dry path when HW INS is engaged). DELTA lets you audition the global difference: the DELTA MODE alert flashes and turns off automatically after 30 seconds of inactivity. S·DELTA arms extended spring-loaded delta: while touching or dragging any module control, you audition only that stage's delta and return to normal audio on release. PEAK CEIL limits sample peaks in dBFS; use a separate true-peak meter if the delivery calls for dBTP.

LINK OUTPUT (chain button on the PEAK, DYN EQ, TONE SCL and DYN BAL rows): when linked, changing the amount, curve or profile of that module moves the OUTPUT knob by the gain the module adds or removes, so you compare at equal loudness and level does not fool you. The correction is computed from the parameters (it does not chase the audio): dynamic modules use the average voice level of the last seconds (−20 dBFS if no voice has played yet), and INPUT is taken into account. Linking does not move OUTPUT; moving it by hand while linked sets a new starting point; unlinking leaves it where it is. Involved controls turn purple; the lit button shows the module contribution (OUT −3.2 dB), red if OUTPUT hits its ±24 dB limit.

A/B memory (A, B and A=B buttons in the OUTPUT STAGE bar): the first time you link, the unlinked state goes to A and you work in B; click A or B to compare (each one keeps all sound parameters, OUTPUT included). The active one stays; A=B ends the comparison by copying it into the other. Memories are saved with the project and are not presets.

Find your way around

Click a module title to show it in MONITOR; the INSERT · FILTER · PREAMP, DRC, DYN EQ (±5 / ±12 dB) and DYN BAL (±6 / ±12 dB) monitors all display the real continuous filter response curves. The LIGHT | DARK buttons switch only the UI color scheme while keeping all controls visible. Hover over a control to read the help line below. The top FULL | MINI | JSFX bar stays pinned at all times: MINI keeps the monitor in view; JSFX exposes the scrollable 58-parameter list for keyboard and screen-reader use. Resizing the REAPER FX window scales the entire interface proportionally.
