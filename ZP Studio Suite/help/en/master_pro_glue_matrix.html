1. Two Mastering Profiles in One Plugin

The header ANALOG | TRANSP switch toggles instantly between two processing architectures while keeping each profile's parameters independent and sharing Tone Guard, Auto dB / Target, Final Safety, meters, and Shared Bus v3.1 telemetry.

TRANSP Path (Transparent — ENHANCE 2× + PRECLIP 2× + Precision Limiter 4× ISP)

INPUTClean drive (-12..+30 dB), no tape saturation.
ENHANCE 2×Upward-density curve (0..100%, up to +7.5 dB) with -48 dB gate, 2.4–6.2 kHz de-harshness guard, and 2× OS shaper.
TONE & PRECLIP 2×Shared Tone Guard + 2× OS transparent soft-clipper (0..100%) shaving fast spikes ahead of the limiter.
PLIM 4× ISPTransparent lookahead limiter, 0..4 dB Soft Knee, Dual-Stage Auto-Release.
OUTPUTShared ceiling, Auto dB, Final Safety, and meters.

ANALOG Path (Colored — Tape Glue + Glue Matrix)

TAPETape glue, controlled saturation, tilt, head bump, and flutter.
COMPMain compressor: threshold, ratio, knee, RMS/Peak, feed mode, and AMU.
GLUEMultistage matrix splitting gain reduction across Macro, Micro, and Crest.
TONEStatic/dynamic Tone Guard and EQ points (with optional Tape Bias).
LIMITERLookahead limiter, Output/Auto dB, and Final Safety.

2. TRANSP Profile: ENHANCE and Precision Limiter

In TRANSP mode, TAPE and COMP/GLUE are completely bypassed. Audio enters clean through INPUT, passes through the upward-density shaper (ENHANCE), optional Tone Guard, and into the lookahead Precision Limiter.

ControlRangeActual DSP Behavior
INPUT-12..+30 dBDrives signal level into ENHANCE, PRECLIP, and the Precision Limiter cleanly, with extended +30 dB range (and Auto/LUFS targets up to -6 LUFS) for loud radio spots and demanding sources.
ENHANCE0..100%Smooth upward-density curve (up to +7.5 dB): lifts low-to-mid program energy without crushing top transients. Features a -48 dB noise-floor gate, a dynamic 2.4–6.2 kHz de-harshness guard (up to -3.2 dB under heavy drive), and a 2× oversampled pre-ceiling shaper to suppress upper-mid aliasing. The LIFT readout displays real average upward lift in dB.
PRECLIP0..100%2× oversampled transparent soft-clipper placed between Tone Guard and PLIM: cleanly shaves short transient spikes (1–3 dB) without aliasing so the Precision Limiter can reach -10 / -8 LUFS without pumping. At 0% it is 100% bypassed; the CLIP readout shows real peak shaving in dB.
ATK0.1..5.0 msPrecision Limiter lookahead window (circular delay buffer with automatic PDC). Values around 1.0..2.0 ms keep fast transients clean without overshoot.
REL5..500 msBase release time of the limiter.
KNEE0..100%Soft limiter knee transition from 0 dB (hard brickwall) up to 4.0 dB (smooth progressive onset).
AUTO RELManual / AutoDual-stage release: isolated peaks recover quickly at the fast REL time without pumping dips, while sustained loud passages engage a slower envelope (3.8×, min 90 ms) to prevent low-frequency intermodulation distortion.

3. 4× True Peak (ISP Guard): Why You Hear the Difference

ISP is not a separate audio coloration stage: it is a global oversampled peak detector driving the Limiter, Final Safety (and Glue/Comp in ANALOG).

A sample-peak limiter (ISP OFF) only sees discrete sample points and allows inter-sample peaks to exceed the ceiling between samples — often by +0.5..+2.0 dBTP on sibilants, plosives, and bright transients. Those inter-sample overs clip consumer DACs and lossy codecs (AAC, Opus, MP3), producing harsh upper-mid grit.

With ISP 4x enabled (default), the detector reconstructs inter-sample peaks via 4× oversampling ahead of the lookahead ceiling: gain reduction tracks the true analog waveform envelope smoothly instead of hard-clipping reconstructed crests. Audibly, highs stay cleaner, transients remain natural, and output stays strictly compliant with -1.0 dBTP even when pushing INPUT or ENHANCE.

4. ANALOG Profile: Glue Matrix & Textures (SM, API, SSA)

The Glue Matrix avoids asking a single compressor to handle every transient at once by splitting gain reduction into three gentle layers:

LayerRole
MACROSlow, musical body compression that holds the master together without chasing individual spikes.
MICROFast, bounded peak control that reduces workload on the final limiter.
CRESTCrest-factor response that steps in progressively when transients protrude too far above RMS body.
ControlPractical Use
COMPEnables/bypasses the main compressor.
GLUEEnables/bypasses the multistage DEP/FRG/micro/crest matrix. When off, only the main compressor runs.
DEPOverall Glue Matrix depth.
FRGFragmentation: higher values shift more work toward Micro and Crest, shielding the limiter from sharp peaks.
TextureThree behavioral profiles (not official hardware emulations): SM (smooth, clean bus glue for podcast/VO), API (quicker, punchier mid grip), SSA (tighter, broadcast-ready cohesion).
Fair Loudness A/B: both ENHANCE (in TRANSP) and GLUE/AMU (in ANALOG) raise perceived loudness by reducing crest factor. Match levels via AUTO or inspect MATCH and LIFT when judging tone and transient cleanliness.

5. NOISE Remote (ZP Audition Noise Monitor in Monitor FX)

The header NOISE strip never injects noise into the Master Bus: it sends remote commands over shared memory (gmem[3670..3683]) to ZP Audition Noise Monitor placed in REAPER's Monitor FX chain.

  • Zero render contamination: simulated environmental noise never enters Master Pro's LUFS/TP/BrownSlope meters and can never print to offline or online renders.
  • 4 RMS-calibrated profiles: CITY (City Car 50 km/h), HWY (Highway 120 km/h), PINK (Pink Room -3 dB/oct), TRAIN (Train / Jet cabin).
  • LUFS-referenced SNR level: -18 / -12 / -6 / -3 dB below the selected loudness target (e.g. at -16 LUFS with -6 dB, noise sits at -22 dBFS RMS) so you immediately hear whether quiet phrases or podcast tails vanish inside a car.
  • Volatile safety state: NOISE always boots OFF when reopening a project and auto-mutes on Stop (PLAY ONLY default).

6. Auto dB, Target & Shared Bus v3.1

TARGET never changes gain; it measures deviation against the selected profile reference. MATCH uses a slow ~4.5 s LUFS/RMS window, ignoring isolated transients. AUTO applies slow trim within MAXTRM: the large readout shows applied trim, while MATCH shows residual error.

Master Pro publishes full telemetry to Shared Bus v3.1 and exposes the SINTESI and CATENA views in its Navigator (including upstream nodes and the 39 ZP Telemetry Engine badge). When Tone Guard is active, its tonal horizon takes priority as the Telemetry Engine reference; when bypassed, the fixed Brown -4.5 dB/oct (±10%) frame applies.
